# Agencies

## UVA Transit

- Run by UVA Parking & Transportation
- Operates the campus bus network (University Transit Service)
- Uses TransLoc for fixed-route and on-demand operations; [TransLoc says the partnership began in 2013](https://transloc.com/case-studies/university-of-virginia-uva/)
- [GTFS feed on Transitland](https://www.transit.land/feeds/f-university~of~virginia); its current static feed URL is hosted on UVA's TransLoc tenant
- The technical publishing system is therefore TransLoc. The UVA owner of the account, feed configuration, and external map-platform relationships is not yet confirmed
- Relevant contacts: [Allison Day](https://parking.virginia.edu/people/allison-day), General Manager, UTS (`and4ne@virginia.edu`), and [Kendall Howell](https://parking.virginia.edu/people/kendall-howell), Assistant Director - Transportation (`klh3t@virginia.edu`)
- Not currently visible in Google Maps or Apple Maps trip planning

## CAT — Charlottesville Area Transit

- Visible in Google Maps; appears in Apple Maps as well
